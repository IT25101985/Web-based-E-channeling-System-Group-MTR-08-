COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about booking, payments, cancellation, and hospital visits.

1. How quickly can I book an appointment?
You can find a doctor, select an available date, choose a time slot, and confirm your booking in under 60 seconds with no complex steps or lengthy forms.
2. How does the Counter Cash payment method work?
Your appointment slot is immediately locked upon booking. Arrive at the hospital 15 minutes before your consultation, present your Booking Reference ID at the reception counter, settle the consultation fee in cash, and collect your official receipt.
3. Can I book an appointment for an elderly parent or someone else?
Yes. When confirming the appointment details, you can enter the patient's full name, NIC/Passport, and contact number so the booking is issued under their name.
4. Can I reschedule or cancel my appointment?
Yes. Log into your Patient Portal, open "My Appointments", and click "Cancel" or "Reschedule". The slot will be automatically released back to the hospital schedule.
5. What should I bring when I attend the hospital?
Please bring your National Identity Card (NIC) or passport, your Booking Reference ID (sent via SMS or visible in your portal), and any past medical records or test reports.
6. How do I receive my booking confirmation?
Immediately upon reservation, your screen displays the Booking Reference ID, and an automated SMS confirmation is delivered to your registered mobile phone number.
7. Are all doctors certified by the Sri Lanka Medical Council (SLMC)?
Yes. Every specialist on MediCare Pro is verified with the Sri Lanka Medical Council (SLMC) and holds active practicing privileges at accredited hospital facilities.
